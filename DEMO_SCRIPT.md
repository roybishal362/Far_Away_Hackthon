# 🎬 Kakehashi — Demo & Pitch Script

> Round 1 accepts a **video OR presentation**. This is the script for a tight **60–90s demo video**
> (judges should understand the whole project from the video alone), plus a 2-minute extended cut
> and the spoken pitch.

## Before you record
- Have it **deployed** (or localhost) and **pre-warm** one run right before recording (Groq cold start).
- Keep a **saved `?plan=` link** of a perfect run open as a backup.
- Use the **persona quick-start chips** so you never type on camera.
- Screen-record at 1080p; speak slowly; let the **live agent timeline** breathe on screen.

---

## 🎯 60-second cut (the one to submit)

**[0:00–0:08] Hook — the problem**
> "In 2025, India and Japan signed a pact to move 50,000 Indian workers to Japan — but the visa journey is a months-long maze. Meet **Kakehashi**: an autonomous AI that walks you through it, grounded in official sources."
*(Show the landing page, scroll the impact band.)*

**[0:08–0:25] The autonomous run**
> "I'm a nurse from Delhi. One click —"
*(Click the **Priya · Nurse** persona chip. Let the **agent timeline stream live**.)*
> "— and a team of agents reads official Japanese sources and live job data, citing every claim. No mockups."

**[0:25–0:40] The depth (tabs)**
> "It gives me a personalized verdict and readiness score… **real live jobs ranked to me**… the official step-by-step with a **government link for every step**… and a study plan with free resources."
*(Quickly click **Overview → Jobs → Procedure**.)*

**[0:40–0:52] The autonomy 'wow' + the proof**
> "Watch what happens for a software engineer —"
*(Click **Arjun · Software Eng** chip.)*
> "— it **refuses** to force SSW and reroutes to the Engineer visa, skipping the steps that don't apply. And it proves itself: grounding takes accuracy from 14% to 71% and **hallucinations to zero**."
*(Open the **Proof** tab / show the chart.)*

**[0:52–1:00] Close**
> "Ask anything, in English, Hindi, or **Japanese** —"
*(Flip the toggle to **日本語**, show a chat reply.)*
> "Kakehashi — a real, autonomous bridge between India and Japan."

## 🎥 2-minute extended cut (add these)
- **Resume upload** auto-fills the form → "it analyses your actual resume."
- **HR persona** → routed to the Specialist visa → "it knows which of three visas fits you."
- **Save & share** → open the link → "a shareable Migration Dossier; download as PDF."

- Say the honesty line: *"If a data source isn't available, it says so — it never fakes data."*

## 🗣️ The 30-second spoken pitch (for judges / Q&A)
> "Kakehashi is an autonomous, source-grounded multi-agent system that guides Indian workers through Japan's
> Specified Skilled Worker journey. It's built for the exact corridor India and Japan signed in 2025. Three
> things make it real, not a wrapper: **every answer is cited from official sources**, the system **measures its
> own accuracy** with a grounded-vs-ungrounded ablation, and it **adapts the plan per person** — even routing
> IT and office workers to the correct, different visa. Real jobs, real government data, in three languages —
> and we're honest about what's live versus roadmap."

## ✅ What to make sure is on screen at least once
- The **live streaming agent timeline** (proves it's agentic, not static)
- A **citation / official link** (proves grounded)
- The **Proof chart** (proves measured)
- The **IT/HR redirect** (proves autonomous decision-making)
- The **日本語 toggle** (for the Japan finale judges)
